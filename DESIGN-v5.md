# La Stazione · V5 brand palette

This is a color refinement of the approved V4 website. The user's supplied logo is the authority: olive lettering, a red cup-and-company mark and warm parchment. The website keeps clean flat surfaces, generous whitespace, the same Fraunces/DM Sans typography, composition, photography and motion.

| Role | Color | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory | `#fbf7ed` | Main canvas and inverse text |
| Warm light surface | `#f1e8d7` | Menu section |
| Olive | `#3f4e2b` | Wordmarks, coffee ribbon, location section, selection states |
| Dark olive ink | `#29351f` | Primary text |
| Muted olive | `#65664f` | Supporting text |
| Cup red | `#b52e20` | Main customer actions and two small floral accents |
| Red hover | `#942416` | Hover on red actions |

The dark sections retain ivory text and artwork. No red content is placed on olive backgrounds. The owner's publish controls stay olive, while destructive operations retain their existing red meaning. The original drawings receive a static CSS hue adjustment toward olive; photos and the logo remain natural. No artificial aging, parchment texture or sepia treatment is added to the page.

The new homepage is `/redesign_v5/`; V1–V4 and the original homepage remain intact. `/menu/` retains its permanent QR destination and uses the current palette. `/owner/` uses the updated neutrals and points to V5. Shared menu data, saved prices, uploaded media, QR files, storage and authentication settings are unchanged.

AA contrast ratios on ivory: primary ink 12.09:1; olive 8.40:1; muted text 5.50:1; red 5.81:1. Muted text on the warmer menu surface is 4.84:1 and red is 5.11:1. Browser verification covers desktop/mobile color states, unchanged layout and behavior, reduced motion, and Safari lifecycle restoration through WebKit.

Editable field borders use `#7c8067`, providing at least 3.36:1 contrast across the relevant surfaces. The batched browser review passed 32 checks and nine accessibility scans at 1440, 390 and 320px. Fifteen representative layout measurements matched V4 at all three widths. A follow-up confirmed the stronger input boundaries. The single Impeccable detector pass surfaced the intentionally logo-derived cream palette and inherited typography/marquee/lightbox heuristics; the user-provided brand and approved composition take precedence.
