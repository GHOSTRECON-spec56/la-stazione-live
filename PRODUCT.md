# La Stazione

La Stazione is a neighborhood coffee shop in Beit Chabab, Lebanon. Its verified public website is https://lastazionelb.com/. The original logo depicts a coffee cup with a heart and two people in red on parchment. Real supplied photography shows illy coffee, pastries, sandwiches, matcha and smoothies, the sunset terrace, and people playing chess and board games.

## Audience and purpose

Local residents and visitors deciding where to have coffee, browse the menu, or find the shop on their phone. Help them explore the complete current menu and get directions. A welcoming, distinctive, modern visual identity should reflect the real place and people.

## Scope and constraints

Create a complete redesign under public/redesign, publicly accessible at /redesign. Preserve every existing landing-page file byte-for-byte. Keep source archives, original media, and spreadsheets private. Use the existing static HTML/CSS/JavaScript stack and real photos. Full accessible menu, mobile navigation, gallery, directions and contact must work. User explicitly authorized download, implementation, and publishing without questions or approval pauses. User will accept local preview if deployment cannot be completed.

## Design scene

A friend chooses a café on their phone in daylight, then checks the menu at the table. The physical reference is La Stazione's red cup-and-company mark, warm café tables, terrazzo, and sunset light. Prioritize clear actions, generously sized text, readable contrast and honest photography.
